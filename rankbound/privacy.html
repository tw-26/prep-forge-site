Rankbound

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

Rankbound: Workout RPG is a workout app published by Elevix Apps. Rankbound does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Information We Collect

None. Rankbound has no accounts, no login, and no sign up. Your nickname, training schedule, workouts, XP, levels, ranks, and history are stored only on your device. Elevix Apps has no servers that receive this data, and we cannot see how you use the app.

Deleting the app removes this data from your device. You can also clear your XP and workout history at any time with Reset Progress in the app's Settings.

No Analytics, No Tracking, No Ads

Rankbound does not include analytics, crash reporting, or advertising, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, or health data. We do not sell or share personal data with anyone.

Children's Privacy

Rankbound does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes To This Policy

If a future version of Rankbound adds features that handle personal data, such as purchases, we will update this policy and revise the effective date at the top of this page before those features are released.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Visit the Support page.
