Daily Quest

Every Workout Is a Quest

Your training day opens as an active quest with clear objectives. Tap each set as you finish it and watch the progress bar fill. Close the app mid-workout and your progress is still there when you come back.

Rewards

Quest Cleared. +100 XP.

Finish every set and the reward sequence kicks in: Quest Complete, an animated XP bar, and a Level Up or Rank Up screen when you earn one. XP is awarded once per day, so your level reflects days you actually showed up.

Progression

Climb From Rank F to Rank S

Seven ranks, each unlocked by level. Tap your rank emblem any time to see every rank, the one you hold now, and exactly how much XP stands between you and the next.

Customize

Build Your Own Routine

Name your quest and add 1 to 10 exercises from a searchable library of 45 bodyweight and cardio moves. Set sets and reps, seconds, or minutes for each one, reorder them, and edit your routine whenever you want more of a challenge. No gym required.

Consistency

Streaks That Respect Rest Days

Choose your training days and your streak only counts those. Rest days never break it. Training History shows your totals, a last-7-days calendar, and every quest you have cleared, so you can track your workouts at a glance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Rankbound free?

Yes. Rankbound is free to download and use, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Do I need an account to use Rankbound?

No. There is no account, login, or sign-up. Pick a nickname and your training days, and you can start your first quest in under a minute. Your progress is stored on your iPhone.

How do XP, levels, and ranks work?

Clearing your Daily Quest earns 100 XP, once per day. XP raises your level, and levels unlock ranks: F (levels 1–4), E (5–9), D (10–19), C (20–34), B (35–49), A (50–74), and S (75+). Rank S takes 318 cleared quests.

What exercises can I do in Rankbound?

Rankbound includes a searchable library of 45 bodyweight and cardio exercises across upper body, lower body, core, and cardio, from push-ups, squats, and planks to burpees, pull-ups, running, and jump rope. Each quest holds 1 to 10 exercises with your own sets, reps, seconds, or minutes.

Is Rankbound good for beginners?

Yes. You choose every exercise, set, and rep count, so you can start small and edit your quest as you get stronger. Early levels come quickly, and rest days on your schedule never break your streak.

Does Rankbound work offline?

Yes. Rankbound works fully offline, so you can train at home, in a park, or at the gym without a connection.

Is there a character or avatar to level up?

No. You are the character. Rankbound tracks your real workouts as quests, and your level and rank grow as you show up and train.
