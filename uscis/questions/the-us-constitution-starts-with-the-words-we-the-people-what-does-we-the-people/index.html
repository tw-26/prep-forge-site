Civics test question 4 of 128 · Principles of American Government

The U.S. Constitution starts with the words 'We the People.' What does 'We the People' mean?

Official USCIS answers

Give any one of these answers.

  • Self-government
  • Popular sovereignty
  • Consent of the governed
  • People should govern themselves
  • Social contract

Why this is the answer

'We the People' means the government's power comes from the people. In the United States, people govern themselves by choosing representatives instead of being ruled by a king or queen.

Answers that are not correct

These wrong answers are common mix-ups. They are not accepted at the interview.

  • The courts write the Constitution
  • Only Congress can govern
  • The President owns the government
  • The states choose all leaders
