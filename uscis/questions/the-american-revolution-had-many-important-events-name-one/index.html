Civics test question 80 of 128 · Colonial Period and Independence

The American Revolution had many important events. Name one.

Official USCIS answers

Give any one of these answers.

  • Battle of Bunker Hill
  • Declaration of Independence
  • Washington Crossing the Delaware
  • Battle of Trenton
  • Battle of Saratoga
  • Valley Forge
  • Battle of Yorktown
  • British surrender at Yorktown

Why this is the answer

The American Revolution included several major events, from early battles to the Declaration of Independence and the British surrender at Yorktown. Remember one accepted event from the official list.
