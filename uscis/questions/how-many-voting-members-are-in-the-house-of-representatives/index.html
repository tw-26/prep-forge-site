Civics test question 24 of 128 · System of Government

How many voting members are in the House of Representatives?

Official USCIS answers

Give any one of these answers.

  • Four hundred thirty-five (435)

Why this is the answer

There are 435 voting members in the House of Representatives. These members come from congressional districts in the 50 states.
