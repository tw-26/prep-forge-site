Civics test question 116 of 128 · Recent American History and Other Important Historical Information

Name one U.S. military conflict after the September 11, 2001 attacks.

Official USCIS answers

Give any one of these answers.

  • Global War on Terror
  • War in Afghanistan
  • War in Iraq

Why this is the answer

After the September 11 attacks, the United States fought conflicts connected to the War on Terror. Accepted examples include the War in Afghanistan and the War in Iraq.
