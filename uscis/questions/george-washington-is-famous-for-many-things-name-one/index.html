Civics test question 86 of 128 · Colonial Period and Independence

George Washington is famous for many things. Name one.

Official USCIS answers

Give any one of these answers.

  • Father of Our Country
  • First president of the United States
  • General of the Continental Army
  • President of the Constitutional Convention

Why this is the answer

George Washington led the colonial army, presided over the Constitutional Convention, and became the first President. These roles are why he is called the Father of Our Country.
