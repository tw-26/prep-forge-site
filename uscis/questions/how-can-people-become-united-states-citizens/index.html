How can people become United States citizens?

Official USCIS answers

Give any one of these answers.

  • Be born in the United States under the conditions set by the 14th Amendment
  • Naturalize
  • Derive citizenship under conditions set by Congress

Why this is the answer

A person can become a U.S. citizen by birth under the 14th Amendment, by naturalization, or by deriving citizenship under rules set by Congress. Naturalization is the process many immigrants use to become citizens.
