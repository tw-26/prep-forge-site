Civics test question 109 of 128 · Recent American History and Other Important Historical Information

During the Cold War, what was one main concern of the United States?

Official USCIS answers

Give any one of these answers.

  • Communism
  • Nuclear war

Why this is the answer

During the Cold War, the United States was mainly concerned about communism and nuclear war. The guide explains that the U.S. wanted to stop the spread of communism around the world.
