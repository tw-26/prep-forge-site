Civics test question 15 of 128 · Principles of American Government

There are three branches of government. Why?

Official USCIS answers

Give any one of these answers.

  • So one part does not become too powerful
  • Checks and balances
  • Separation of powers

Why this is the answer

The Founders separated government power into three branches because they feared one person or group could become too powerful. Separation of powers and checks and balances help prevent that.

Answers that are not correct

These wrong answers are common mix-ups. They are not accepted at the interview.

  • So judges can make budgets
  • So the President can serve longer
  • So states can ignore Congress
