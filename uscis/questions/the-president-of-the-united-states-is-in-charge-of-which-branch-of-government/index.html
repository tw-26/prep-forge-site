Civics test question 17 of 128 · System of Government

The President of the United States is in charge of which branch of government?

Official USCIS answers

Give any one of these answers.

  • Executive branch

Why this is the answer

The President leads the Executive Branch. This branch enforces federal laws and includes the Vice President, Cabinet, departments, and agencies.
