What is Veterans Day?

Official USCIS answers

Give any one of these answers.

  • A holiday to honor people in the U.S. military
  • A holiday to honor people who have served in the U.S. military

Why this is the answer

Veterans Day honors people who have served in the U.S. Armed Forces. It is observed on November 11, the date World War I ended in 1918.

Answers that are not correct

These wrong answers are common mix-ups. They are not accepted at the interview.
