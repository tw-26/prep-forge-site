Civics test question 62 of 128 · System of Government

What is the capital of your state?

Official USCIS answers

Give any one of these answers.

  • The capital of the applicant's state or territory
  • D.C. is not a state and does not have a capital

Why this is the answer

This answer depends on the applicant's state or territory. Washington, D.C. is not a state and does not have a state capital.
