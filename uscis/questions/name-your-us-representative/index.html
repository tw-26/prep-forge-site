Civics test question 29 of 128 · System of Government

Name your U.S. representative.

Official USCIS answers

Give any one of these answers.

  • The applicant's current U.S. representative
  • D.C. or the applicant's territory has no voting representative in Congress
  • The applicant's district currently has no voting representative in Congress

Why this is the answer

This answer depends on the applicant's congressional district. A representative serves people in a district; some territories have nonvoting delegates, and a vacant district may have no voting representative at that time.
