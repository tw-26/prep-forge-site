Civics test question 70 of 128 · Rights and Responsibilities

What is one way Americans can serve their country?

Official USCIS answers

Give any one of these answers.

  • Vote
  • Pay taxes
  • Obey the law
  • Serve in the military
  • Run for office
  • Work for local, state, or federal government

Why this is the answer

Americans can serve their country in many ways. The guide connects service with duties like voting, paying taxes, obeying the law, public work, military service, and running for office.

Try it yourself

Pick the correct answer.

Answers that are not correct

These wrong answers are common mix-ups. They are not accepted at the interview.

  • Only vote in local elections
  • Ignore laws
  • Avoid taxes
  • Serve on the Supreme Court
