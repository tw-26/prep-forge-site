Civics test question 118 of 128 · Recent American History and Other Important Historical Information

Name one example of an American innovation.

Official USCIS answers

Give any one of these answers.

  • Light bulb
  • Automobile
  • Cars
  • Internal combustion engine
  • Skyscrapers
  • Airplane
  • Assembly line
  • Landing on the moon
  • Integrated circuit
  • IC

Why this is the answer

The official list accepts several examples of American innovation. If you remember one example such as the light bulb, airplane, assembly line, moon landing, or integrated circuit, that is enough.
