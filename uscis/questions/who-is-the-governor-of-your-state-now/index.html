Civics test question 61 of 128 · System of Government

Who is the governor of your state now?

Official USCIS answers

Give any one of these answers.

  • The applicant's current state governor
  • D.C. does not have a governor

Why this is the answer

This answer depends on the applicant's state and can change after elections. D.C. is not a state, so D.C. residents should answer that D.C. does not have a governor.
