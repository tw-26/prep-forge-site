Civics test question 95 of 128 · 1800s

What did the Emancipation Proclamation do?

Official USCIS answers

Give any one of these answers.

  • Freed the slaves
  • Freed slaves in the Confederacy
  • Freed slaves in the Confederate states
  • Freed slaves in most Southern states

Why this is the answer

The Emancipation Proclamation announced that enslaved people in most Southern states were free. The guide notes that many were not actually freed until after the Civil War ended.

Answers that are not correct

These wrong answers are common mix-ups. They are not accepted at the interview.

  • Declared independence from Britain
  • Created the Supreme Court
  • Ended World War II
  • Gave women the right to vote
