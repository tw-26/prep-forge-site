What is the name of the Speaker of the House of Representatives now?

Official USCIS answers

Give any one of these answers.

  • The current Speaker of the House of Representatives

Current answer: Mike Johnson

Current as of May 26, 2026. USCIS asks for the person serving on the day of your interview.

Why this is the answer

The Speaker is the leader of the House of Representatives. This answer can change, so applicants must know who is serving as Speaker at the time of their interview.
