What does the judicial branch do?

Official USCIS answers

Give any one of these answers.

  • Reviews laws
  • Explains laws
  • Resolves disputes about the law
  • Decides if a law goes against the U.S. Constitution

Why this is the answer

The Judicial Branch reviews and explains laws and resolves legal disputes. It can also decide whether a law goes against the Constitution.

Answers that are not correct

These wrong answers are common mix-ups. They are not accepted at the interview.
