Civics test question 23 of 128 · System of Government

Who is one of your state's U.S. senators now?

Official USCIS answers

Give any one of these answers.

  • One of the applicant's current U.S. senators
  • D.C. or the applicant's territory has no U.S. senators

Why this is the answer

This answer depends on where the applicant lives and who currently serves in the Senate. D.C. and U.S. territories do not have U.S. senators because they are not states.
