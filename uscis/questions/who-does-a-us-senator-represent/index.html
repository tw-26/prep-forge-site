Who does a U.S. senator represent?

Official USCIS answers

Give any one of these answers.

  • Citizens of their state
  • People of their state

Why this is the answer

A U.S. senator represents the people of their state. Senators are elected statewide, unlike House members who represent districts.

Answers that are not correct

These wrong answers are common mix-ups. They are not accepted at the interview.

  • All U.S. citizens
  • Citizens in their congressional district
  • The President
  • Supreme Court justices
