Frequently Asked Questions

Is Citizen Ready free to download?

Yes. The app is free to download and includes a free preview. An optional in-app purchase unlocks Full Access, including Spanish study mode.

Is there a free online version?

Yes. You can take the free practice test and read all 128 questions and answers on this website. The website doesn't save progress; the app does.

Does the app cover all 128 official USCIS civics questions?

Yes. The app includes the full 2025 USCIS civics question bank, organized into topics so you can track what you have answered and what still needs work.

Can I study in Spanish?

Yes. Spanish study mode lets you learn in Spanish while still seeing the official English test wording. This feature requires an in-app purchase.

Does the app give state-specific civics answers?

Yes. Practice questions reflect your state, district or territory, including your current U.S. representative, governor, senators and capital.

Is Citizen Ready affiliated with USCIS or the U.S. government?

No. Citizen Ready is published by an independent company, Elevix Apps, and is not affiliated with USCIS or the U.S. government.

What is the Pass Guarantee?

If you purchased the app, studied with it, and did not pass the civics portion of your interview, you can contact support with proof of purchase and your test result. Eligible pass guarantee requests are reviewed within 7 business days.

Is Citizen Ready available on Android?

Not yet. The app is available on iPhone and iPad. On Android, you can use the free online practice test.
