The 2025 civics test has 6 questions about Symbols, numbers 119 to 124 on the official USCIS list. Each one below shows the accepted answers. Open a question for a plain-English explanation.

  3. 121. Why does the flag have 13 stripes?

    • Because there were 13 original colonies

  4. 122. Why does the flag have 50 stars?

    • We all become one
